Descriptions

Short (about 50 words)

Browser of the Dead is a top-down survival horror shooter for Android from solo studio Zed Head Games. One flashlight, a pistol, and whatever is left of the city: twelve levels, boss fights, an endless Horde mode, twin-stick touch aim and four difficulties. Coming soon to Google Play for $2.99.

Long

The door didn’t hold. You start in a house that is already lost, and the street outside is worse. Twelve places stand between you and the end of this: a cemetery, a boarded police station, a flooded basement, the sewers, an army bunker. Keys are hidden. Doors stay shut until you find them. Some things only move when they hear you.

Browser of the Dead is a top-down survival horror shooter where your flashlight cone is all you get. Past it, the room is gone. Along the way you face the Giant Zombie Turtle, the Laser Turtle, a tank on your tail and the Chimera Commander waiting at the end. When the story is done, Horde mode has no keys and no exit: just a clock, a score, and a street that gets meaner the longer you last.

It plays with twin-stick touch aim (or classic controls with aim assist) and supports gamepads. Progress saves at the start of every level, and four difficulties run from Easy to Nightmare, which unlocks after you beat the game on Hard. The synth soundtrack was made for the game. Art and audio were created with the help of AI tools.

Browser of the Dead is made by one developer, David Riha, under the name Zed Head Games. It is in closed testing on Google Play now and will sell for $2.99.

Features

  • 12 levels: city streets, a cemetery, an abandoned house, a police station, a flooded basement, the sewers, an army bunker and a C4 escape
  • Boss fights: the Giant Zombie Turtle, the Laser Turtle, a tank pursuit and the Chimera Commander
  • Endless Horde mode with a clock and a score
  • Twin-stick touch aim, or classic controls with aim assist
  • Controller support: left stick moves, right stick aims
  • Save and continue, with a checkpoint at the start of every level
  • Four difficulties: Easy, Normal, Hard and Nightmare (unlocks after beating Hard)
  • Synth soundtrack made for the game
