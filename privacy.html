Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026. This policy covers the mobile game Browser of the Dead, published by Zed Head Games (David Riha).

What the game stores

The game keeps progress, lives, ammo, achievements, campaign completion, and Horde high scores on your device only (local storage). That data is not sent to Zed Head Games or to a server.

What we do not collect

  • No account, login, or email is required to play.
  • No advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.
  • No precise location.

Controller support uses the device’s built-in gamepad connection. That stays on the device.

How to delete your data

Uninstall the app, or clear the app’s storage in your phone’s settings. That removes saved progress and scores.

Children

The game is a survival-horror title with violence and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change. If ads, accounts, or analytics are added later, this page will be updated before those features ship.

Questions about this policy: zedheadgames.dev@gmail.com.
