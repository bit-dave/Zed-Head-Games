Earlier teaser

The door didn’t hold.

You start in a house that is already lost. The street outside is worse. Twelve places stand between you and the end of this: a cemetery, a boarded police station, a flooded basement, the sewers, an army bunker. Keys are hidden. Doors stay shut until you find them. Some things only move when they hear you.

The long way out

City, graveyard, house, station, sewer, bunker. Each floor has its own lock, its own dead, and an exit you cannot skip.

What the light misses

The cone is all you get. Past it, the room is gone. In the water, a footstep is loud enough to bring them.

The Horde

No keys. No exit. A clock, a score, and a street that gets meaner the longer you last.

What’s inside

  • 12 levels

    City streets, a cemetery, a police station, the sewers, an army bunker and a C4 escape.

  • Boss fights

    The Giant Zombie Turtle, the Laser Turtle, a tank on your tail, and the Chimera Commander waiting at the end.

  • Endless Horde mode

    No keys and no exit. Just a clock, a score, and a street that gets meaner the longer you last.

  • Twin-stick touch aim

    Move with one thumb and aim with the other, or keep the classic controls with aim assist. Your choice in Settings.

  • Controller support

    Connect a gamepad: left stick moves, right stick aims.

  • Save & Continue

    Progress is checkpointed at the start of every level, so you can pick up right where you left off.

  • Four difficulties

    Ease in, or prove yourself. Beat the game on Hard to unlock Nightmare.
